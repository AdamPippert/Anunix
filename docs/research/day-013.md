## Implementation

The policy validator accepts bonuses from 0 through 1000 and penalties from -1000 through 0.
Cost divisors must be between 1 and 1000.
These limits are project guardrails, not experimentally selected tuning values.

Snapshot validation checks the engine count, class, status, derived readiness, and topology ranges.
Resource weights and quality scores must follow their declared 0 through 100 range.
The simulator also validates the cell's routing strategy, locality, and declared topology range.
These bounds keep scores and margins within signed 32-bit arithmetic.

All validation precedes output initialization.
A rejected candidate leaves the prior simulation result unchanged.
Simulation does not modify or activate the incumbent policy.

The [native regression](../../kernel/core/tools/research_day013.c) rejects 20 malformed inputs.
It checks result preservation and verifies that each rejected policy remains unchanged.
A valid alternate policy changes the simulated winner.
Replaying the incumbent then reproduces the original result.

## Limits

This change validates a simulation API; it does not implement live kernel tuning or policy activation.
The live planner still uses its existing constants.
No Linux tuning mechanism, policy compiler, signed revision, or live rollback protocol is added.
The fixture uses synthetic engines and establishes deterministic validation behavior, not workload performance gains.
The guardrails constrain valid candidates; they do not establish that a candidate improves an operating workload.
